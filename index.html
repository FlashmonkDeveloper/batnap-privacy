Privacy Policy

Batnap: Snore & Sleep Tracker · Effective 13 November 2026

Batnap listens to your night on your phone and keeps what it learns on your phone. There are no accounts, no servers, no analytics and no ads. Your nights and recordings never leave your phone, and we could not hear or see them even if we wanted to.

What we collect

Nothing. Batnap does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal, health or audio data. The app has no sign-in, does not connect to any server of ours, and contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting software. It holds the internet permission only because Google Play's billing software needs it for purchases; Batnap's own code never uses the network.

How the microphone is used

Batnap uses the microphone only while you are tracking a night or a nap, doing the wind-down breathing exercise, or running the phone check. While it is listening, Android shows its microphone indicator and Batnap shows a notification.

  • Sonar. The phone plays a tone too high to hear and listens to its echo to follow your breathing and movement.
  • Night sounds. It listens for snoring, talking, coughing and other sounds, and for noise in the room. Sounds are named by a sound-recognition model that is built into the app and runs on the phone.
  • Analysed as it arrives. Sound is processed in the phone's memory. The night is not recorded as a whole.

Recordings

If "Keep sound moments" is turned on, Batnap saves a few short clips each night, each about ten seconds long, such as your loudest snore or a moment of sleep talking. A clip can include other sounds in the room, including other people's voices. Clips are kept in the app's private storage, where other apps cannot read them. Turn the setting off in Settings to stop saving clips.

What stays on your phone

Batnap stores the following in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Your nights and naps: start and end times, breathing rate, movement, snoring, sleep talking, breathing pauses, named sounds, room noise and sleep score
  • The tags you add to nights, such as "Side sleeping"
  • Any sound clips you have chosen to keep
  • Your settings, such as theme, alarm time, sleep goal and the result of the phone check

How it is protected

  • Private storage. Everything is kept in the app's own storage area, which Android keeps separate from other apps.
  • No cloud copies. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for Batnap, so the system does not copy your nights or recordings to Google or any other cloud.
  • Nothing is sent. Batnap has no server to send to, and its own code makes no network connections.

Things you choose to share

  • Night card. You can share a picture summarising a night through any app on your phone.
  • Doctor report. You can create a PDF summary of your last 30 nights, for example to take to an appointment.

These leave your phone only when you send them, to whoever you choose. We never receive a copy.

Backups you create

With Plus you can save every night and kept recording to a single backup file, wherever you choose, and restore from it later. The file is not encrypted, so anyone who has it can read it; keep it somewhere you trust. We never receive a copy.

Partner mode and the widget

If you turn on partner mode, the names you type and each person's nights are stored on the phone like everything else. The optional home screen widget shows last night's score and snoring minutes on your home screen, where anyone looking at your phone can see them.

Permissions and why they are used

  • Microphone: to hear the sonar echo and the sounds of the night, as described above.
  • Notifications: to show that tracking is running, and for the backup alarm.
  • Run in the foreground and keep the phone awake: so tracking continues through the night with the screen off.
  • Vibration: for the alarm and the short buzz when tracking starts or stops.
  • Internet and network state: required by the Google Play Billing library for purchases. Batnap itself sends nothing.
  • Google Play billing: to offer Batnap Plus and the optional tip.
  • Biometrics (fingerprint or face): only if you turn on "Lock recordings". The check is done entirely by Android; Batnap never receives or stores your fingerprint or face.
  • Audio settings: to set the media volume the sonar tone needs while tracking, and put it back afterwards.

Batnap also reads the phone's motion sensor to notice when the phone is turned face down or lifted. It does not use your location, contacts, camera or files.

Google Play services

Batnap asks the Google Play Store app whether a newer version is available so it can prompt you to update, and the Rate Batnap button opens the app's Google Play page. These are handled by Google Play under your Google account; Batnap sends none of your nights or recordings to Google.

Purchases

Batnap Plus (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and the optional tip are bought through Google Play, which handles the payment under your Google account. Batnap never sees your card or payment details; it only learns from Google Play whether Plus is active on this phone. Google's billing software may send Google technical information about purchases, under Google's privacy policy. Free users can record sound moments for their first seven days; after that, recording is part of Plus.

Deleting your data

You can delete a single night, with its recordings, from that night's report, or delete everything in Settings. You can also have nights and recordings removed automatically after a period you choose. Uninstalling Batnap permanently deletes everything it stored on your phone. Night cards or reports you shared are separate copies that you can delete yourself.

Not medical advice

Batnap is a wellness tool. Its figures, including breathing rate, breathing pauses, snoring and sleep score, are estimates. It is not a medical device, it does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and it does not replace advice from a qualified health professional.

Children

Batnap is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Your responsibility

Because your data lives only on your device, keeping it private is in your hands. Anyone who can unlock your phone can open Batnap and play your recordings. If you share a bedroom, tell the people you share it with that Batnap listens at night and may keep short clips, or turn "Keep sound moments" off.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled, for example if online features are added, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
